2️⃣ What EXACTLY comes under each notebook?
This is important. Don’t mix things.

📘 01_data_cleaning.ipynb

Purpose: Make raw data usable and trustworthy.
What you MUST do here:
Load dataset (using relative path)
Remove garbage rows (Ticker, NaN rows)
Drop useless columns (level_0, index)
Rename columns properly
Convert Date to datetime
Set Date as index
Check missing values
Save cleaned data (optional but good)
What NOT to do here:
❌ Plotting
❌ Forecasting
❌ Interpretation
This notebook is purely data preparation.

📘 02_eda.ipynb
Purpose: Understand the data behavior visually.
What you MUST do:
Summary statistics (describe)
Line plot of Close price vs Date
Histogram of Close price
Box plot of Close price
Volume trend over time
Questions you answer:
How does price behave over time?
Is data skewed?
Any extreme values?
What NOT to do:

❌ Model training
❌ Forecasting
EDA ≠ prediction.

📘 03_stability_volatility.ipynb
Purpose: Show analytical thinkig (important).
What you MUST do:
Calculate daily returns
Rolling mean (20/30 days)
Rolling standard deviation (volatility)
Identify stable vs unstable periods
Plots:
Rolling volatility over time
Close price + rolling mean
Interpretation:
When is the market stable?
When does volatility spike?
This notebook gives depth to your project.

📘 04_forecasting.ipynb
Purpose: Predict future trend (simple, honest).
What you MUST do:
Split data (train/test or time-based)
Use ONE simple method:
Moving Average OR
Linear Regression
Forecast short horizon (20–30 days)
Plot actual vs predicted
MUST include:
Limitations section
No fake accuracy claims
Simple model + clear explanation > fancy ML.
